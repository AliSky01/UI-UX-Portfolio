Appointment Policies and Conditions
Appointment Booking
  • Appointments may be scheduled through the ClinicSense web-based system by the patient or authorized guardian.
  • All bookings are subject to availability and must be reviewed and approved by the clinic’s receptionist or administrator.
  • Patients must provide complete and accurate information when scheduling an appointment.
Consultation Fee
  • A ₱1,000 consultation fee is required for every scheduled appointment.
  • Payment will be made at the clinic.
  • Appointments may not be finalized without acknowledgment of the consultation fee.
Guardian Salary Verification
  • Guardians may be required to provide proof of financial capacity (e.g., salary or income details) for verification purposes.
  • This ensures that the patient’s medical care, treatment, and related expenses can be properly supported.
  • All submitted financial information will be handled with strict confidentiality.
Confirmation of Appointment
  • Appointments are considered confirmed only after approval by the clinic.
  • A notification will be sent once the appointment is approved or if further adjustments are required.
Arrival and Grace Period
  • Patients are advised to arrive at least 10–15 minutes before their scheduled appointment.
  • A grace period of 20–30 minutes is given. Failure to arrive within this timeframe may result in:
    • Automatic cancellation or voiding of the appointment
    • Reassignment of the slot to another patient
Cancellation and Rescheduling
  • Cancellation or rescheduling must be done at least 24 hours before the appointment.
  • Requests are subject to availability and clinic approval.
  • Late cancellations may affect future bookings.
No-Show Policy
  • Patients who fail to attend their appointment without prior notice will be marked as “No-Show.”
  • Repeated no-shows may lead to:
    • Temporary restriction from booking
    • Requirement for manual approval of future appointments
Appointment Modifications
  • The clinic reserves the right to reschedule or cancel appointments due to emergencies, doctor or staff unavailability, or operational concerns.
  • Patients will be notified promptly in such cases.
Guardian Responsibility
  • Guardians booking on behalf of patients must ensure that all submitted details are correct and that the patient is available on the scheduled date and time.
  • Guardians may also monitor appointment status and updates through the system.
Data Privacy and Confidentiality
  • All personal and medical information provided during appointment booking is handled in compliance with data privacy regulations.
  • Information is accessible only to authorized clinic personnel.
Compliance with Clinic Rules
  • Patients and guardians are expected to follow clinic protocols and policies during visits.
  • Failure to comply may result in denial of service or cancellation of appointments.

This document summarizes the clinic's booking policies and conditions. For questions or the full legal text, contact the clinic administration.
