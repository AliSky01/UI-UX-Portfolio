How We Started

Estrellas' Homecare Clinic Inc. is a private healthcare facility located on Kamias Road, Quezon City. We specialize in compassionate, holistic care for elderly patients and individuals with psychiatric needs, providing family-like support through licensed physicians, skilled nurses, physical and occupational therapists, and psychological interns. We focus on patient-centered care, collaboration, and continuity.

Estrellas' Homecare Clinic was established in 1980 by a dedicated and compassionate nurse whose vision was to provide accessible, high-quality healthcare to the community. With a strong commitment to patient care and service excellence, she laid the foundation for a clinic that prioritizes trust, empathy, and professionalism.

Our Mission

To provide safe, respectful, and evidence-based homecare and residential services that enhance the physical, mental, and emotional well-being of our patients through compassionate, multidisciplinary support.

Our Vision

To be a trusted community partner and a center of excellence for geriatric and psychiatric care, delivering accessible, dignified, and sustainably managed healthcare services that inspire hope and improve quality of life for all families.

Our Core Values

Compassion

At Estrella's Homecare Clinic, compassion is at the heart of every interaction. We treat each patient with kindness, empathy, and respect, ensuring they feel heard, valued, and cared for throughout their healthcare journey.

Reliability

We are committed to providing consistent, dependable, and high-quality healthcare services. Patients and their families can trust Estrella's Homecare Clinic to deliver timely care, accurate information, and continuous support whenever it is needed.

Patient-Centered Care

We prioritize the unique needs, preferences, and well-being of every patient. Estrella's Homecare Clinic ensures that care plans are personalized, involving patients and their families in decision-making to achieve the best possible health outcomes.
